Mission 02 · UCI Concrete Compressive Strength · 1,030 laboratory tests

Abrams' law,
107 years later

In 1918 Duff Abrams said one number sets the strength of concrete: water divided by cement. We test how far that law goes, and what the recipe adds for mixtures the model has never seen.

08 · Conclusion

A 1918 law still does most of the work

Findings

Limits

Data pool several laboratories; curing conditions not recorded
Repeated tests of a mixture are correlated: analytic SEs optimistic
Recipes were not randomized: slag and fly-ash effects are associations
exp(predicted log f) estimates the median, not the mean
